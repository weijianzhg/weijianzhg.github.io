Recent experiments: [a local 2B email-triage model](https://notesfromzero.substack.com/p/dont-drive-a-ferrari-to-buy-milk), [an autonomous loop for prompt-injection classifiers](https://github.com/weijianzhg/autoresearch-classifier), and [Deeper Level](https://deeperlevel.xyz/), a chess interface that shows the model’s candidate moves and critique.
