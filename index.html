01Open-source CLILocal models

Tuned Tensor

Spec-driven fine-tuning for small open models.

One file describes the model: its base model, rules, examples and how it is scored. The tt agent proposes each change as a diff you approve, then trains, evaluates and serves the model on your own GPU or AWS account.

  • Local training, evaluation and serving need no account
  • Base and tuned models are scored on the same test cases
  • Email triage case study: 61.5% → 89.0% pass rate with a 2B model

02Public betaTrading workflows

Hedge Layer

Run your trading routines from WhatsApp.

Traders describe their own rules in plain words, run them on request or on a schedule with live data, and keep a journal of decisions to review later. It is read-only: it never trades and never tells you what to buy.

  • WhatsApp, plus a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux
  • Data from Polymarket, Hyperliquid, SEC EDGAR, World Bank and ECB
  • MCP server and HTTP API for your own agents and scripts

03LiveGames for AI

Deeper Level

Play strategy games against AI models.

Play chess, or Fortune, a small economy of land, production and sealed auctions, against ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok, and read each model's reasoning as you play. Next, games that players choose to share will become reinforcement learning data for training smaller models.

  • Chess: see the move, the alternatives it rejected and what it expects next
  • Fortune: you against three AI settlers over six or eight rounds
  • Sharing games for research is off by default
